Invoicing in the new CRM: a build backlog

Development items pulled from the Aug 24 billing discovery meeting. Two invoicing processes run today — both almost entirely by hand in Excel and QuickBooks. This is the candidate list of what the new CRM should absorb, for the group to review and prioritize.

Source  CRM Dev — Invoicing, 8.24.26 Attendees  Bill · Maria · Lili · Erika · Kirby Status  For review

Standard clients

owner: Maria · QuickBooks + Excel

  • Weekly master order report (Fri→Thu) reconciled against the monthly client report.
  • Bills off "plan paid"; a per-client bank transfer every Friday.
  • Month-end: weeklies + transfers must tie to the monthly invoice.
  • Discrepancies come from cancellations, deletes, and silent price changes.

RxCompass

owner: Lili · Excel per Compass spec

  • Biweekly submission file, columns formatted to Compass's layout.
  • Compass pays per prescription / per order, not per invoice.
  • Reconciles ~40 payment emails back to Rx numbers by hand.
  • Wrestles unit-of-measure (mL, auto-injectors), bad NDCs, 90-day insurance lag.
The through-line: get client contracts + formulary set up right, add a real audit trail (no hard deletes), and most of this manual reconciliation disappears. Everything below hangs off those two foundations.

Reconciliation & audit trail

REC-1

No hard deletes — soft-cancel only

Transactions can be canceled but never deleted. The current CRM lets anyone delete or re-price with no restriction — the root cause of month-end discrepancies.

raised repeatedly — top pain point

High
REC-2

Full audit trail

Who changed what and when on every transaction and price — so a discrepancy can be traced instead of hunted.

from Maria — "no way to see what changed"

High
REC-3

Automated weekly ↔ monthly reconciliation

from Maria — the recurring month-end effort
